    02 / Construction

    What the casing rating means on site.

    EN 1886 grades how an AHU casing performs once the unit is built. Luftek's 502LK series is rated at or near the top class on all five properties.

    Property502LKWhat it does for you
    Casing leakageL1Conditioned air stays in the unit. Less fan energy is spent on air that never reaches the room.
    Filter bypassF9Air goes through the filter, not round it. That matters most in clean rooms and operating theatres.
    Thermal bridgingTB2The thermal-break aluminium frame keeps the outer skin from sweating in humid coastal climates.
    Thermal conductivityT2The injected PU panels hold the coil's cooling inside the casing.
    Casing strengthD1Panels hold their shape under high fan static pressure, so seals stay tight over years.
    • A

      Extruded aluminium frame

      With thermal break. Stays square and corrosion-free.

    • B

      50 mm injected PU panels

      Precoated outer skin, galvanised inner. Rockwool, aluminium and stainless steel are options.

    • C

      Direct-drive plug fans

      AC or EC motors, AMCA 210 / 300 certified. IE2 / IE3, IP55, class F.

    • D

      Stainless steel drain pan

      Sloped for full condensate removal. Nothing stands, so nothing grows.

    03 / Certification

    Tested by third parties, not just in our brochure.

    Consultants can specify Luftek against the same performance standards they use for European and American brands.

    Eurovent
    AHU performance and casing mechanical characteristics, certified by the Eurovent certification body.
    AHRI 410
    Water coils certified for cooling and heating performance, so the duty on the datasheet is the duty on site.
    AHRI 1061
    Recovery effectiveness of the wheels, plate exchangers and heat pipes in the energy recovery range.
    AMCA 210 / 300
    Fan air performance and sound ratings.
    VDI 6022 · DIN 1946-4
    Hygiene requirements for clean room units in hospitals, pharma and food processing.
    ISO 16890 / EN 779
    Certified filter classes.
    ESMA
    Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology rating for roof top package units.
    ISO 9001:2015
    Quality management system covering design and manufacture.
