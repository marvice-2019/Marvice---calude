Inside the casing

What makes a Luftek AHU hold its rating.

Tap a point on the Clean Room Unit to see how it is built.

  1. AC or EC motor, no belts to wear. Fans certified to AMCA 210 for performance and AMCA 300 for sound.

  2. AHRI 410 certified chilled water and hot water coils, so the duty on the datasheet is the duty on site.

  3. Injected PU foam between a precoated outer skin and a galvanised inner skin. Rockwool, aluminium and stainless steel are options.

  4. Extruded profiles with a thermal break, rated TB2 under EN 1886, so the casing does not sweat in humid climates.

  5. Sloped for complete condensate removal. No standing water means nothing grows inside the unit.

  6. ISO 16890 / EN 779 certified filters, sealed to an F9 filter bypass class so air cannot slip round them.
