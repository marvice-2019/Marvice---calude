Overview

For longer duct runs and higher system resistance. Rated at 75 Pa external static pressure, with UL-recognised motor components and low-noise centrifugal DIDW blowers in a slim casing for hotels, hospitals and apartments.

Salient features

  • Heavy-gauge sheet metal construction
  • Optimum design with low-height construction
  • Motors manufactured with UL-recognised components
  • Motors with overload protection temperature sensors
  • Low-noise aluminium impeller, centrifugal DIDW blowers
  • Counter-flow design for high-efficiency cooling and heating coils
  • Multiple row-deep options for 2-pipe and 4-pipe operation
  • Cooling coils for high Delta-T district cooling
