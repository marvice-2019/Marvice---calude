Overview

The Smart Unit ships with an inbuilt control panel, control valve and sensors. Its intelligent controller saves energy by modulating the fan and the chilled water valve to the actual load. The unit is BMS compatible over MODBUS or BACnet, and the HMI lets the client set and view parameters at the touch of a button.

Salient features

  • Inbuilt control panel, control valve and sensors
  • Fan speed and chilled water modulated to load
  • BMS integration over MODBUS or BACnet
  • HMI to set and view parameters at the unit
  • EN 1886 casing ratings certified by Eurovent
  • Casing complying with EN ISO 9223
  • AHRI 410 certified cooling and heating coils
  • Fans certified to AMCA 210 (performance) and AMCA 300 (sound)
  • ISO 16890 / EN 779 certified filters
  • EC fans available for energy saving
