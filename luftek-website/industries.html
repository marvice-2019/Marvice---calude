Hospitals

Operating theatres, ICUs and isolation rooms need air that stays clean all the way to the room. That means hygienic casings with no standing water, filters that air cannot bypass, and casings that do not leak.

  • Hygienic casing to VDI 6022 and DIN 1946-4
  • Filter bypass class F9 for theatre-grade filtration
  • Stainless steel drain pans: no standing water
  • Quiet fan coil units for wards and patient rooms
