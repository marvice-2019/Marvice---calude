Who we are

Quality, innovative air conditioning products since 2017.

Luftek® was established in 2017 with a vision to serve the HVAC industry with air conditioning products that meet any design requirement for better indoor air quality, while staying energy efficient, eco-friendly, compact and affordable.

A state-of-the-art manufacturing facility near Chennai, with modern machinery and manufacturing processes, produces quality HVAC products for commercial, industrial, hospital, pharma and special applications.

Our engineering team has comprehensive knowledge of engineering standards and requirements. Product design complies with EN and AHRI standards for mechanical and cooling performance, and every unit can be customised to the project's construction, size and performance requirements.

Vision

To meet customer requirements with sustainable and eco-friendly solutions by delivering quality products consistently.

  8. 2024New factory

Manufacturing plant

Cheyyar SIPCOT, Tamil Nadu.

Our new factory, inaugurated in 2024, sits in the Cheyyar SIPCOT Industrial Complex Phase II. It builds AHUs, FCUs and package units for projects across India, the Gulf and Sri Lanka.

Research & development

Great ideas need intensive research.

Our investment in R&D supports our commitment to innovation. Highly skilled engineers dedicated to AHU design keep finding new ways to improve Luftek machines.

The team spans electrical and mechanical engineering, fluid dynamics, design, testing, acoustics and controls. That range of backgrounds lets us approach each challenge from every angle, and design AHUs and heat recovery units that achieve optimum performance.
