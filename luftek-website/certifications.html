Standards

Eight standards, one product range.

01

Eurovent

AHU performance and casing

Luftek air handling units are certified by the Eurovent certification body for the performance of the unit and the mechanical characteristics of the casing construction.

02

AHRI 410

Cooling and heating coils

Luftek water coils are tested to AHRI 410 and certified for cooling and heating performance. The AHRI Certified® mark assures buyers that the equipment will perform accurately and consistently.

03

ISO 9001:2015

Quality management

To demonstrate its ability to provide products and service that meet customer requirements, Luftek maintains a quality management system in accordance with ISO 9001:2015.

04

AHRI 1061

Energy recovery

Energy recovery equipment in Luftek ERUs is certified to AHRI 1061 for recovery effectiveness.

05

AMCA 210 / 300

Fans

Fans used in Luftek AHUs are certified to AMCA 210 for air performance and AMCA 300 for sound.

06

ISO 16890 / EN 779

Filters

Filters are certified to ISO 16890 / EN 779 classifications.

07

VDI 6022 · DIN 1946-4

Hygiene

Clean Room Units are designed to DIN 1946-4 and VDI 6022 hygiene requirements, with VDI 6022 certified accessories.

08

ESMA

Package units

Roof top package units are certified by the Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology for higher efficiency and lower power consumption.

EN 1886 explained

What the casing classes mean on site.

EN 1886 rates an assembled AHU casing on five properties. Class 1 is the best in each. These are the ratings of Luftek's 502LK series.

PropertyClass rangeLuftek 502LKWhat it means
Casing strengthD1 – D3D1How far panels deflect under fan pressure. Less deflection keeps seals tight for years.
Casing leakageL1 – L3L1Air lost through the casing. L1 means conditioned air reaches the room instead of the plant room.
Filter bypass leakageF9 – G1F9Air that slips round the filter. F9 is the tightest class, essential for clean rooms.
Thermal conductivityT1 – T5T2Heat passing through the panels. A lower class keeps the coil's cooling inside the unit.
Thermal bridgingTB1 – TB5TB2Cold spots through the frame. A lower class stops the outer skin sweating in humid climates.

503LK series: thermal bridging TB3. 251LK series: thermal conductivity T3.
