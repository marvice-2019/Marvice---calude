What it does

From the first minute to the paid invoice.

One-tap timer

Start, switch and stop timers by client and project from desktop or phone. Forgot to start one? Add the block afterwards in seconds.

WhatsApp nudges

Anyone with a missing day gets a reminder at 6 pm and can reply with their hours. Managers see who is behind without chasing.

GST-ready invoices

Approved billable hours become invoices in INR with 18% GST shown separately, or in GBP for UK clients. Export to your accountant in one click.

Client profitability

Hours spent against what each retainer pays, with team cost per hour applied. See which accounts to reprice before renewal.

Retainer burn alerts

Set the hours a retainer covers. Hourhand warns the account lead at 80% and 100%, before the overrun becomes free work.

Utilisation by person

Billable versus non-billable time for each team member and department, week by week, with approvals built in.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is Hourhand for?

Agencies, studios, consultancies and IT service teams of 5 to 200 people who bill clients by the hour or on retainers and need to know where time goes.

Does Hourhand handle GST invoicing?

Yes. Approved billable hours turn into invoices in INR with 18% GST shown separately, or in GBP and other currencies for clients outside India.

Can my team log time from WhatsApp?

Yes. Hourhand sends a daily WhatsApp nudge to anyone with a missing timesheet, and they can reply to log hours without opening the app.
