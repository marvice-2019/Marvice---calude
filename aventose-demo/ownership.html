Questions

Good to know.

How far does the S110 go on a charge?
About 100 km on road with the standard 2.4 kWh LFP battery, and about 130 km with the extended-range 3.2 kWh NMC battery.
Where can I charge it?
Anywhere with a regular plug point. Lift the battery out from under the seat and charge it at home, at work or at your shop with the portable charger. Fixed charging and battery swapping are also supported.
How long does charging take?
80% in about 4 hours and a full charge in about 6 hours, according to the latest specification sheet.
Can it handle waterlogged roads?
Yes. The motor, controller and battery are IP67 certified, and the VCU and display are IP65 rated against rain and dust.
What is the warranty?
The battery is covered for 8 years or 1,00,000 km, and the motor for 3 years or 50,000 km. The S110 is ARAI certified for a two lakh kilometre service life.
Can I take a test ride?
Yes. Book one online and the team will bring an S110 to you.
