Built rugged

Waterlogged road? Keep going.

IP67 certified motor, controller and battery to handle waterlogged roads and rough terrain. IP65 rated VCU and display to shrug off rain and everyday dust.

  • Rugged 42 mm parallel tubular chassis with cross members
  • Hydraulic suspension with twin rear shocks
  • High ground clearance for every kind of road
ARAI certified

Two lakh kilometres of service life.

ARAI certification for the S110 came with an industry-first two lakh kilometre service life. The frame is engineered to absorb bad-road shocks for years, not months.
