02 — Rugged by design

Built for the road
you didn't choose.

Most EVs are designed for brochures. The S110 is designed for Tuesday morning on a road nobody has fixed since 2019.

/ 01

A trellis frame stress-tested over cobbles, ruts and kerbs, with long-travel suspension that keeps the tyre on the ground.

/ 02

Monsoon-sealed electrics

IP67-rated battery and motor connectors. Ride through knee-deep water the way you already do — just without the worry.
