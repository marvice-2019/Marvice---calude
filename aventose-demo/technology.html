Battery · Powered by T.E.J.A.S design

8 years.
1,00,000 km.

The 2.45 kWh LFP pack carries an 8-year / 1,00,000 km warranty. Four layers of engineering make that possible.

  • Advanced thermal conduction and heat dissipation
  • Thermal runaway protection
  • Intelligent BMS senses and monitors cell and battery parameters
  • Predictive algorithms to safeguard and extend battery health
  • CAN-based communication for a wide array of data
  • Reduced cell junction ohmic losses
  • Robust cell junction contact
  • Short circuit protection
  • Component design, selection and placement for greater heat dissipation
  • Bolstered aluminium enclosure for greater strength
  • Pressure release vent

  • Controlled drive-mode transitions for smooth operation
