Our focus

High-adoption electric mobility.

Electric two-wheelers only change things when ordinary riders can own one without compromise. That is why Aventose focuses on every part of the ecosystem that decides adoption: safety, flexible charging, ruggedness and reliability.

  • Charge anywhere, with a removable battery
  • Safe, with IP67 sealing and Safe Mode
  • Rugged, with an ARAI-certified 2,00,000 km service life
  • Multi-purpose, for families, shops and fleets
