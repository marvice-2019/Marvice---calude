Questions

What happens if my calendar stops syncing?

Bookings pause for that calendar, you get an alert, and the light turns red until you reconnect. Nobody can book a time we can’t check.

Can I move my existing booking links over?

Yes. Recreate your event types in a few minutes, then swap the link in your bio. There’s no automatic importer yet.

Do you send WhatsApp reminders?

Yes, on Solo and Duo, with 300 or 600 a month included.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Plan › Cancel. One tap. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Who answers support?

A person, within one working day.

Is GST included?

No. Prices are shown before GST, and the invoice shows the 18% separately with your GSTIN.
