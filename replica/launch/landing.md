## 2. The problem

Two themes, paraphrased from 190 reviews of the leading scheduler, not quoted:

- **Your scheduler says you're free when you're not.** A calendar quietly stops syncing, a client books over another session, and you find out when two people turn up.
- **When it breaks, nobody answers.** You get a bot, a help article, or a script. And cancelling the plan turns out to be harder than signing up.

## 3. How it works

1. **Connect your calendars.** Google, Outlook or iCloud. Each one gets a sync light.
2. **Share your link.** Put it on WhatsApp, Instagram or your site. Clients pick a time you're actually free.
3. **Show up.** The booking lands in your calendar with the video link, and both of you get a reminder.

## 6. FAQ

- **What happens if my calendar stops syncing?** Bookings pause for that calendar, you get an alert, and the light turns red until you reconnect. Nobody can book a time we can't check.
- **Can I move my existing booking links over?** Yes. Copy your event types into Hourhand in a few minutes, then swap the link in your bio. (An importer is not built yet, so this is a manual move for now.)
- **Do you do WhatsApp reminders?** Yes, on Solo and Duo, with 300 or 600 a month included.
- **How do I cancel?** Settings › Plan › Cancel. One tap. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
- **Who answers support?** A person, within one working day.
- **Is GST included?** No. Prices are shown before GST, and the invoice shows the 18% separately with your GSTIN.
