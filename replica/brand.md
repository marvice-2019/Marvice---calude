# Brand: Hourhand

The angle comes from `replica/fixes.md`: *for solo coaches, tutors and service businesses tired of a scheduler that gets more complicated with every update and never puts them through to a person, the booking link that syncs every calendar it touches, and real people answer when it doesn't.*

## 1. Name

**Recommended: Hourhand.** The hour hand is the part of a clock that keeps steady time and holds its position, which is the reliability promise. It's a real English word, easy to spell after hearing it once, and nothing in it echoes "Calendly" (no "cal", no "-ly", no "-endly").

## 2. Palette

Calendly's brand is a saturated blue (`#006bff`). Hourhand uses a warm saffron on paper, a different colour family entirely, and it reads well for an Indian audience.

| role | hex | use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bg | `#fffdf8` | warm paper |
| surface | `#f7f1e8` | cards, booking panel |
| border | `#e8dfd2` | dividers |
| border-input | `#8a7f70` | form fields, 3.86:1 against bg |
| text | `#1f1a14` | body, 16.99:1 against bg |
| text-muted | `#62584b` | secondary text, 6.85:1 against bg |
| accent | `#b4410e` | burnt saffron: primary buttons, links, the logo hand. White on it is 5.67:1 |
| highlight | `#f2a93b` | marigold, decoration only, never used for text |
| danger | `#b42318` | errors |
| success | `#1b7a4b` | confirmed bookings |

The contrast check ran on `replica/design/tokens.json`: **12 pairs, 0 failing AA**. Fonts are Fraunces for display and Inter for body, both free and open-source (OFL).

## 3. Logo brief

- **The idea:** a clock face reduced to a single short hand, pointing up-right like a tick, so it reads as both "the time is set" and "confirmed".
- **Mark type:** a symbol plus a wordmark, with the symbol able to stand alone as the app icon.
- **Symbol:** a filled circle in accent `#b4410e` with one off-white hand from the centre at about 1 o'clock, and a small marigold dot at the pivot. No numerals, no minute hand.
- **Wordmark:** "hourhand" in lowercase Fraunces SemiBold, text colour `#1f1a14`.
- **Must work at 16px** (the favicon is just the circle and hand) **and as a 1024px app icon** (full bleed, no transparency).
- **Deliverables:** SVG (symbol, wordmark, lockup), a 1024×1024 iOS app icon with no alpha channel, a favicon set (16, 32, 180 and 512 px), and a 1200×630 social image.
- **Must not resemble Calendly's mark.** No blue, no blue-and-cyan pairing, no ring or "C" letterform, no rounded-square badge in their style. Put Calendly's logo next to the drafts and check.

## 4. Voice

**Three words**

- **Plain**, not dumbed down. Short words, no jargon, and the user still feels respected.
- **Steady**, not stiff. Calm when something breaks. It says what happened and what will happen next.
- **Human**, not cute. It sounds like a capable front-desk person: no forced jokes, no emoji in errors.

**Do and don't**

| do | don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Your Google Calendar stopped syncing at 4:10 pm. We've paused bookings so nothing double-books." | "Oops! Something went wrong 😅" |
| "Cancel anytime. One click, no call." | "Manage your subscription in Settings › Billing › Plan › Manage." |
| "A person will reply within one working day." | "Our AI assistant can help with that!" |
| "Booked: Thu 14 Nov, 3:00 pm IST (your time)." | "Your meeting is scheduled for 15:00." with no zone |
| "Pick a time that works." | "Leverage our scheduling solution to optimise your availability." |
