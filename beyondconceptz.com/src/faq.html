Which areas do you build in?

Chennai, Kanchipuram and nearby towns in Kanchipuram and Chengalpattu districts. If your plot is further out, send us the location and we will tell you honestly whether we can supervise it well.

Is the rate per sq.ft my final price?

The collection rates are a guide. After the site visit and design you receive an itemised estimate with every material, brand and quantity. Once signed, that estimate is your price.

Do you take care of plan approval?

Yes. We prepare the submission and structural drawings and follow them through with the authority. Government fees are paid by the owner and shown separately.

How do payments work?

An advance to start the design, then payments tied to construction stages. You pay for each stage after it is complete and you have seen it, on site or on video.

I live outside India. Can I build with you?

Yes. Design reviews happen on video calls, and you get photos, video and the engineer's checklist at every stage. Most owners visit only for the foundation and the handover.

Can I hire you only for design or interiors?

Yes. Each service can be taken on its own. Tell us what you need below and we will quote only for that.
